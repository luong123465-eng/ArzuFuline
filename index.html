A LETTER TO SPRING · NO. 001

Dear sakura,
please wait for me.

Somewhere between childhood dreams and the person I’m becoming, I left a little wish beneath the cherry blossoms.

one day, with someone special ♡

🌸 I think I’ve loved sakura for as long as I can remember

When I think about the kind of beauty that makes me stop and look twice, I always come back to cherry blossoms. There is something so gentle about them: the pale pink petals, the way a branch seems to hold a little cloud of flowers, and the way the wind can carry them away before you are ready to say goodbye. I love that they never need to be loud to be unforgettable.

Ever since I was little, I’ve imagined what it would feel like to see a whole street lined with sakura in spring. In my mind, the sky would be soft and bright, the air would be cool, and petals would drift around like tiny pieces of a dream. I would probably walk slowly, look up far too often, and try to remember every small detail because I wouldn’t want the moment to end.

Maybe that is why cherry blossoms feel a little bittersweet to me. They are beautiful precisely because they do not stay forever. A petal lands on your sleeve, another disappears into the breeze, and suddenly you understand that even a short moment can become a memory you keep for years. I like that quiet feeling — the sweetness, the longing, and the little ache of wishing time would pause.

And if I could choose one simple dream to make real, it would be this: standing beneath a cherry blossom tree with the person I love. No grand confession, no perfect movie scene, no need for expensive gifts. Just the two of us, close enough to share the same view, watching petals fall around us. Maybe we would talk about silly things; maybe we would stay quiet. I think either would be enough, as long as we were there together.

I want to look at that person and feel that the world has become gentle for a little while. I want to remember the breeze, the pink petals in their hair, and the way an ordinary afternoon can turn precious because of who is standing beside you. If that day ever comes, I hope I won’t rush it. I hope I’ll let the moment settle in my heart, like a letter I can reread whenever life gets noisy.

So yes, I’m a person who likes coding and making things — but I’m also someone who keeps a soft little dream about sakura. I want to keep learning, keep creating, keep watching stories that make me feel something, and someday find myself beneath those blossoms with someone special. Until then, I’ll keep the wish safe. Some dreams are lovely even before they come true. ♡
